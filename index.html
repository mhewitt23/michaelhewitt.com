I sing

I’ve been on professional stages since I was 12 years old. I left the stage world during the pandemic when I realized I’d hit all of the goals I’d set for myself, and the shutdown was the excuse my ego could accept for me to leave.

My primary focus now is on building my practice and serving my clients, but I still train and perform regularly. Let’s be real: If nothing else, I love singing and am obsessed with the voice.

I create

My primary creative project has been and continues to be myself, becoming the man I admire and offering him to the world. Anything else I create is downstream of that aim.

In 2015, I interviewed one of my professors who had recently co-authored a book on creativity. This interview changed my perspective on creativity forever, exposing me to the idea that creativity was anything that’s within you that you bring forth.

Philosophy

My mission is to help people reclaim the agency they’ve lost, so they can go do what they were built for.

I want to live a life of simplicity and discipline.

What holds up a house? Pillars.

Intention

Speak so that others will win, not so that you’re validated. Be of service or be of self.

Creativity

Creativity is an invitation to connect. Create from a State of Play.

The body

Your body is your business card. An awesome body should complement your life, not run it.

It is not enough to ask yourself better questions. It is not enough to answer them courageously. They’re only as valuable as what you do with the answer. You have one life. Consider the best case scenario as much as the worst. Your good old days are right now. What would you do if you knew you could not fail? If you don’t go for that, you’ll regret it on your deathbed. Take more shots. Become who you needed but did not have. Give that person to the world. You can be of service or be of self, but you can’t be both. Choose. Go.
